About Us

Together for a Healthier, Safer, and Brighter Tomorrow

A Maryland-licensed Residential Service Agency providing dignified, quality skilled nursing and home care to children and adults of all ages.

Our Mission

Dignified Care, Delivered at Home

Awesome Healthcare Solutions LLC exists so people of every age can receive skilled, compassionate care without leaving the comfort of home. We combine licensed clinical expertise with genuine warmth — because you matter.

We proudly serve Anne Arundel County, the Baltimore area, Prince George’s County and all of Maryland, in partnership with P&A Health Services LLC.
